No database: eight JSON files

There is no database and no server. The "tables" are eight JSON files in data/: six edited by hand, two generated by a command. All are checked by npm run check, then copied into the script at build time. Each file wraps its content in one key, next to a free _readme note for editors. Row counts are those of 2026-11-17.

FileHoldsRows
territories.jsonThe Confederation (CH) and the 26 cantons; a canton is covered once it has parties, and a covered canton names its parliament as it does itself ("Grand Conseil", "Parlement" in the Jura)27
parties.jsonParties per territory, left to right: the order of the array is the order on screen. All 51 verified; 42 carry their presidency (7 national parties, 35 cantonal sections)51
state-sites.jsonOfficial sites per territory, the canton's own site first. All 15 verified15
tools.jsonDecision aids, valid for every territory. All verified7
parliament-current.jsonThe current federal composition, generated by npm run parliament and never edited by hand: the 255 active persons as the Parliament's API returned them (200 National Council, 46 Council of States, 7 Federal Council, the Chancellor and the Vice-Chancellor), with 16 Federal Council mandates; apart, the mapping of 9 API party labels to CH parties; the request, terms, attribution and download date255
cantonal-parliaments.jsonThe latest election of each covered cantonal parliament, generated by npm run cantonal-parliaments and never edited by hand: OFS table T 17.02.05.01.03, sheet "actuel (2021-2026)", its 19 column labels, the row of each of the 6 covered cantons and the 34 notes, unchanged; apart, the mapping of OFS columns to each canton's parties; the request, asset, attribution, licence and download date6
cantonal-governments.jsonThe governments of the covered cantons, kept by hand: 29 members in GE, VD, VS, NE and JU, each with party, department, role, the official page, the page naming the party and the check date. Fribourg has no entry until its new Conseil d'État is published5
ballot.jsonThe next ballot: 4 items, 34 sourced party recommendations (yes, no or free vote), Federal Council recommendation on every item, 6 cantonal entries (3 cantonal items), 18 sources1

Nested in these rows: 10 previous URLs and 10 party facts (founding years), none with a source yet. The OFS 2023 seats file and the hand-kept Federal Council seat facts were removed on 2026-11-17: the Parliament's data replaces both.

Decisions on this layer

Settled
  • The data lives in one JSON file per referential, not in code.PRD-0001.US-09
  • Every link records its status, check date, evidence and previous addresses.PRD-0001.US-10
  • A canton is available as soon as it has parties in the parties file.PRD-0001.US-09
  • Only verified links reach the voter; to_confirm and not_found are internal to the maintainer.PRD-0001 §8PRD-0001.US-10
  • Figures from an official source live in their own generated file, apart from the hand-edited referential, and are never edited by hand; check requires 200 National Council and 46 Council of States members and 7 Federal Council members.PRD-0001.US-16
  • The Parliament's data is stored unchanged, the mapping to the site's parties apart; PLD maps to the PLR and Al to the Greens.Research-0002, rulings 3 and 4
  • Only names, party, canton and function are stored for a person.Research-0002, ruling 7
  • Cantonal parliaments are stored as elected, from the OFS table, with the election year; the OFS data unchanged and its mapping to the cantons' parties apart.PRD-0001.US-17PRD-0001 §8
  • Governments and presidencies are kept by hand, each with its source and check date; a party whose site names nobody has no presidency entry.PRD-0001.US-14PRD-0001.US-18PRD-0001 §8
  • The left-right order stays indicative and editorial; every party is shown. Published scores back it for the national parties and their sections, and the footer names the survey.PRD-0001 §8PRD-0001.US-06
  • Facts without a source are kept in the data but not shown.PRD-0001 §8PRD-0001.US-06
  • Party recommendations exist only for national parties and only with a listed source.PRD-0001.US-03
  • The data is embedded at build time; nothing is fetched at run time.PRD-0001.US-12
