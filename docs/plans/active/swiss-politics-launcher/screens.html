What the voter sees

Five pages, one territory sheet and one menu, all at phone width (390 px), captured from the built site. Every page reads the territory chosen once (US-05) and shows only what concerns it. The copy is the real French copy of the site.

Ballot list (US-01)

Home page. The date and a countdown, the number of items, when the voting material arrives, then the numbered items with their question. With a canton chosen, a line names its cantonal items or links its voting page.

{{FIGURE:ui-01-ballot-list|Phone screen: header with menu button, territory bar set to Confédération, "Dimanche 29 novembre 2026, dans 13 jours", title "4 objets à trancher", and a numbered list of ballot items with their questions|Ballot list, Confederation}}

Ballot item: Comprendre (US-02)

One item, three tabs. The first gives what a yes and a no change, the Federal Council and Parliament recommendation when recorded, the note "Résumé indicatif. La brochure officielle fait foi.", then links to the official page, the brochure, easyvote and Vimentis. Back and next buttons move between items.

{{FIGURE:ui-02-item-understand|Phone screen of ballot item 1 of 4 on the AVS and VAT, tab Comprendre selected, showing the block "Si vous votez OUI"|Item page, tab Comprendre}}

Ballot item: Comparer (US-03)

The national parties as a half-circle, left to right, coloured yes, no or "À confirmer". Only recommendations with a source under the chart are coloured. Then smartvote, smartmonitor and Swissvotes.

{{FIGURE:ui-03-item-compare|Phone screen, tab Comparer: a half-circle of nine national parties, PS and Verts in the yes colour, solidaritéS and PLR in the no colour, the others grey, with a legend Oui, Non, À confirmer|Item page, tab Comparer}}

Ballot item: Décider (US-04)

The practical steps (material received, postal vote or vote at the commune, whom to ask), then the voting page of the chosen canton and ch.ch.

{{FIGURE:ui-04-item-decide|Phone screen with territory Genève, tab Décider: numbered steps to check the voting material, vote by post or at the commune, and contact the chancellery|Item page, tab Décider, Geneva}}

Territory sheet (US-05)

Opened by the territory bar on every page. "Confédération", then a tile map of the 26 cantons: covered cantons selectable, the others "Bientôt" and disabled, a dot on cantons with a cantonal item on 29 November. Closes by the close button, the backdrop or Escape, and gives focus back to the territory bar.

{{FIGURE:ui-05-territory-sheet|Bottom sheet "Choisir le territoire" over the parties page: a Confédération button and a tile map of the 26 cantons, six of them dark (available), Genève highlighted, Valais with a dot, with a legend Disponible, Bientôt, Objet cantonal le 29 nov.|Territory sheet}}

Parties (US-06)

One sector per party, left to right in the order of the data file, the chosen party and its rank in the centre. The card shows the name, a line of description, the position, the facts that have a source, the lines of who sits for the party (next blocks), and the party site, or a search link when no site is known. A fact without a source is not shown: the founding years still have none, so no card shows them.

{{FIGURE:ui-06-parties|Phone screen with territory Genève: half-circle of nine Geneva parties, PS selected (3 sur 9), arrow buttons Plus à gauche and Plus à droite, and the top of the card of PS Genève: Position Gauche, Présidence Amanda Gavilanes et Cyril Mizrahi, Conseil d'État 2 membres sur 7, Grand Conseil (élection 2023) 18 sièges sur 100|Parties of Geneva, card of the third party}}

Party card of the Confederation (US-15)

Right after the position, the presidency: the name, or the names of a co-presidency ("A et B"), kept by hand from the party's own site. Then three lines from the Parliament's data, current composition: its Federal Council members by name ("Aucun membre" otherwise), its National Council and Council of States seats. Members elected under a regional label are named with their canton ("dont 1 PLD Bâle-Ville", "dont 1 Al Zoug"). Under the card, the sources: "Présidence : plr.ch, vérifié le 17.11.2026. Élus : Services du Parlement de l'Assemblée fédérale, Berne, données du 17.11.2026". A party whose site names nobody (PST-POP, solidaritéS) shows no presidency line.

{{FIGURE:ui-13-party-card-ch|Phone screen of the card Parti libéral-radical: Position Centre droit, Présidence Susanne Vincenz-Stauffacher et Benjamin Mühlemann, Conseil fédéral Ignazio Cassis and Karin Keller-Sutter, Conseil national 27 sièges dont 1 PLD Bâle-Ville, Conseil des États 12 sièges, the presidency and Parliament source line and a link Site du parti plr.ch|Card of the PLR, Confederation}}

Party card of a canton (US-15)

A cantonal section's card follows mockup board 4: the presidency, then its seats in the canton's government as a count only ("2 membres sur 7", so it does not read as the rank shown in the half-circle; the line takes the body's own name, "Gouvernement" in the Jura), in the Grand Conseil with the election year ("18 sièges sur 100"), and among the canton's members of the National Council and the Council of States ("3 sur 12", "1 sur 2"). A sourced zero is written out ("Aucun siège sur 100"); a line without data is not shown: a party the OFS does not count separately, Fribourg's government until it is recorded. The sources under the card name one link per site.

{{FIGURE:ui-14-party-card-ge|Phone screen of the card PS Genève: Position Gauche, Présidence Amanda Gavilanes et Cyril Mizrahi, Conseil d'État 2 membres sur 7, Grand Conseil (élection 2023) 18 sièges sur 100, Conseil national (GE) 3 sur 12, Conseil des États (GE) 1 sur 2, then a source line naming ps-ge.ch, ge.ch, the OFS and the Parliament with their dates, and a link Site du parti ps-ge.ch|Card of PS Genève}}

Qui gouverne et qui siège: Confederation (US-13)

The Federal Council first: the 7 members with party and department, the President then the Vice-President marked with the API's labels, the others by name, a summary by party, and the Federal Chancellor on a line of their own. Department and function names are shown as the Parliament gives them, unchanged.

{{FIGURE:ui-10-institutions-ch|Phone screen "Qui gouverne et qui siège" for the Confederation: Conseil fédéral, 7 membres, with Guy Parmelin UDC marked Président/e de la Confédération and Ignazio Cassis PLR marked Vice-président/e, each with the full name of the department|Institutions, Confederation, Federal Council}}

Then the National Council as a half-circle proportional to its 200 seats and the Council of States as a bar of its 46 seats, both left to right in palette B2, each with a legend of seats per party. Parties without a colour of their own fold into "Autres", whose legend names them; regional labels are named ("PLR (dont 1 PLD)"). The charts are decorative for screen readers: the legend carries the figures. Under them, the Parliament's attribution with the download date, the statement that the site is not official, and a link to parlament.ch.

{{FIGURE:ui-11-institutions-ch-chambers|Phone screen of the National Council: a half-circle of 200 seats in eight colours, Verts on the left and UDC on the right, 200 sièges in the centre, and a legend Verts (dont 1 Al) 23, PS 41, PVL 11, Centre 29, PEV 2, PLR (dont 1 PLD) 27, UDC 62, Autres (MCG 2, UDF 2, Lega 1) 5|Institutions, Confederation, National Council}}

Qui gouverne et qui siège: a canton (US-14)

First the government, kept by hand: its name in the canton, a summary by party, then each member with party, department and, for the president and vice-president, the role as the official page writes it. The sources name one link per site with the oldest check date. Fribourg shows no government until its new Conseil d'État, elected on 29 November 2026, is published and recorded.

{{FIGURE:ui-12-institutions-ge|Phone screen for Geneva: Qui gouverne et qui siège, Conseil d'État, 7 membres : 2 PS, 2 PLR, 1 Verts, 1 Centre, 1 LJS, then Anne Hiltpold PLR with her department and Présidente du Conseil d'Etat in red, Carole-Anne Kast PS Vice-présidente du Conseil d'Etat, Nathalie Fontanet PLR, Thierry Apothéloz PS, Pierre Maudet LJS|Institutions, Geneva, Conseil d'État}}

Then the Grand Conseil as elected: a half-circle proportional to its seats, labelled with the election year from the OFS table, left to right in palette B2. The canton-only party with the most seats takes the eighth colour, magenta (MCG in Geneva); the others fold into "Autres", named in the legend. A note says the composition may have changed since the election, and the OFS is named with the table and its download date.

{{FIGURE:ui-15-institutions-ge-grand-council|Phone screen of the Grand Conseil of Geneva: 100 sièges, résultat de l'élection de 2023, a half-circle in seven colours with 100 sièges in the centre, a legend Verts 15, PS 18, Centre 9, PLR 22, MCG 14, UDC 12, Autres (LJS 10) 10, and a note that the composition may have changed|Institutions, Geneva, Grand Conseil}}

Last, the canton's members of the Council of States and of the National Council, by name, grouped by party from left to right, then the canton's Grand Conseil site when the referential has one.

{{FIGURE:ui-16-institutions-ge-bern|Phone screen for Geneva: the end of the Grand Conseil legend and its OFS source, then Les élus du canton de Genève à Berne, Conseil des États (2) Carlo Sommaruga PS and Mauro Poggia MCG, Conseil national (12) grouped by party, Verts, PS, Centre|Institutions, Geneva, members in Bern}}

Decision aids (US-07)

Three steps: Comprendre, Comparer, Décider. Each tool shows its name, what it does and its host; the last step links the territory's voting page and the next ballot.

{{FIGURE:ui-07-tools|Phone screen "Aides à décider", step 1 Comprendre with cards for Vimentis, easyvote and Année politique suisse, each with a CH badge and its host|Decision aids, Geneva}}

Official sites (US-08)

The official sites of the territory in one list, with a search that ignores accents and case. An empty result says so.

{{FIGURE:ui-08-state-sites|Phone screen "Sites officiels" for the canton of Geneva, the search box holds "grand" and one card remains: Grand Conseil, ge.ch/grandconseil|Official sites of Geneva, filtered}}

Menu (US-08)

A hamburger on a phone, open on a computer. Two groups: "Je veux..." (the three pages) and "Voir ce que fait l'État": "Qui gouverne et qui siège" (the institutions page, which replaced the external "Trouver un élu" link), then Lire une loi, Voir les résultats, Consulter les chiffres and Tous les sites, whose links follow the territory.

{{FIGURE:ui-09-menu|Phone screen with the menu open: group Je veux with three page links, group Voir ce que fait l'État with Qui gouverne et qui siège, three external shortcuts to ge.ch, ge.ch/elections and bfs.admin.ch, and Tous les sites|Menu, Geneva}}

Decisions on this layer

Settled
  • Mobile first: the voter's device is a phone; the menu folds into a hamburger on a phone and is open on a computer.PRD-0001 §5
  • The Confederation design system gives the look, without its coat of arms or logos; the site says it is independent and not official.PRD-0001 §5
  • A recommendation is coloured only when sourced; every other party reads "À confirmer".PRD-0001.US-03
  • A party fact appears only with a source and a check date.PRD-0001.US-06PRD-0001 §8
  • A party with no known site gets a search link, never a guessed address.PRD-0001.US-06PRD-0001.US-10
  • Uncovered cantons stay on the map, marked "Bientôt" and disabled.PRD-0001.US-05
  • Keyboard access to the half-circle, the map and the sheet, ARIA labels on sectors and tiles, focus returned on close.PRD-0001 §5
  • Editorial tone: airy, numbered lists, no long dash in any text.PRD-0001 §5
  • "Qui gouverne et qui siège" replaces the external "Trouver un élu" link in the menu.PRD-0001.US-13
  • Party colours follow palette B2, at most 8 per chart; a party keeps its colour on every page and the others fold into "Autres"; a chart always comes with its legend and figures.PRD-0001 §8
  • The federal chambers show the current composition, the National Council as a half-circle proportional to its seats; the parties half-circle stays one equal sector per party.PRD-0001.US-13Research-0002, ruling 1
  • Every figure from the Parliament carries its attribution and download date, and the page states the site is not official.PRD-0001.US-13Research-0002, ruling 3
  • Names, party, canton and function only, for the Federal Council and a canton's members in Bern.Research-0002, ruling 7
  • PLD counts with the PLR and Al with the Greens, and the card or legend names them.PRD-0001.US-15Research-0002, ruling 4
  • A section whose data is not sourced yet is not shown.PRD-0001.US-14
  • A Grand Conseil is shown as elected, labelled with the election year, with a note that its composition may have changed; in each one the canton-only party with the most seats takes the eighth colour.PRD-0001.US-14PRD-0001 §8
  • A cantonal party's card shows its government seats as a count, its Grand Conseil seats with the election year and its share of the canton's federal delegation; a sourced zero is written out, a line without data is absent.PRD-0001.US-15PRD-0001 §8
  • The presidency line comes right after the position, names a co-presidency "A et B", and its source and check date are named under the card.PRD-0001.US-15PRD-0001 §8
  • Fribourg's government stays hidden until the official result is published and checked.PRD-0001.US-18
