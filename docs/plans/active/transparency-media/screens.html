What the voter sees

One new page, "À qui appartiennent les médias", reached from a new "Transparence" group of the menu. The screens are captured from the built site (dist/, opened from disk) at phone width (390 px) by capture-screens.ts, with the data of data/media.json as checked on 2026-11-18. They follow the owner-approved mockup v3; where the site differs (no "Maquette" banner, Soleil in the site's own SVG, the "Éditeur" line), the PRD decides (next tabs). The fög box of ui-05 comes with US-22 (increment 16).

Menu: the Transparence group (US-20)

A third group between "Je veux..." and "Voir ce que fait l'État": "Savoir à qui appartiennent les médias" (short label "Médias", a "nouveau" badge) and "Voir les lobbies (bientôt)", greyed, not focusable, until phase 2.

Médias: Réseau (US-21)

A switch Réseau / Soleil, a line on how to use the view, a search by name (media, company or person) with its list of names. Réseau shows every holder, minority holders included: an arrow from owner to owned with the share, a dashed arrow where sources disagree. One shape and colour per kind (media red, company grey, person or family violet, public body blue, association or foundation green), named in the legend. The graph pans and zooms by touch inside its box; the page itself never scrolls sideways.

{{FIGURE:ui-02-media-network|Phone screen: switch with Réseau selected, a hint, a search field with a Voir button, and a tall box with the ownership network: red media boxes, grey companies, violet ellipses for people, blue diamonds for public bodies, green boxes for associations, linked by arrows with shares, laid out by fcose and turned to fill the tall box; a legend under it|Réseau, the whole network}}

Médias: Soleil (US-21)

Three rings: in the centre the kind of top owner (colour only, no label), in the middle the top owner, at the edge the media. Labels only on the outer rings and only on sectors large enough to read; the owner found large central labels unreadable on a phone. Tapping a sector zooms in; the centre zooms out. The legend names the colours of the kinds.

{{FIGURE:ui-03-media-sun|Phone screen: switch with Soleil selected, a sunburst whose outer ring names the media, the middle ring their top owners such as Famille fondatrice de TX Group, Schweizerische Radio- und Fernsehgesellschaft, BNJ Media Holding, shortened to fit, and a thin unlabeled centre ring coloured by kind around a white centre; legend Personne ou famille, Association fondation, Société sans propriétaire publié|Soleil, three rings}}

Card: when sources disagree (US-20, US-24)

Tapping a node, a sector or a name in a list opens the same card. For a company: "Détenu par" with each holder and its share as published, "part non publiée" otherwise; "Détient"; how many media it reaches; the source and its check date. When two sources give the same share to two holders (Léman Bleu: 24,66 % to ESH Médias on one Medienmonitor page, to Philippe Hersant on another), both are listed, each with a note saying the sources disagree. A person is shown with name and share only.

{{FIGURE:ui-04-media-card-disputed|Card of the company TV LEMAN BLEU SA, opened by the search: SA, Carouge (GE), its UID, sourced to the register; Détenu par Stéphane Barbier-Mueller 50,75 %; ESH Editions Suisses Holding SA 24,66 %, sources en désaccord, with a note that the ESH page of the Medienmonitor attributes this share to ESH Médias and the page of other brands to Philippe Hersant; Philippe Hersant 24,66 %, sources en désaccord, with the same note; Ville de Genève 17,06 %; each with its source checked on 18.11.2026; Édite Léman Bleu|Card of TV Léman Bleu SA}}

Card: the fög index (US-22)

"Tonalité des votations": the value on a scale from −100 to +100 with a marker, then what it measures in the fög's own words (61 federal ballots, 2018 to 2025, centre-left to centre-right proposals), the media average (−4) and "Ce n'est pas une position politique du média", with its source and date. A title the fög does not measure says "Pas de mesure du fög pour ce média". The words "gauche" and "droite" never appear as a badge.

{{FIGURE:ui-05-media-card-fog|Card of 24 heures, Média Presse: Éditeur Tamedia Publications romandes SA, société anonyme, Lausanne, sourced to the register and the imprint; Propriétaire TX Group AG (100 %); Au sommet Famille fondatrice de TX Group, sourced to the TX Group annual report; Voir la fiche de l'éditeur; Signaler une erreur. The box Tonalité des votations (value -5 on a scale from -100 to +100, media average -4) joins it with US-22|Card of 24 heures}}

Lists: who links several media, every medium (US-20, US-21)

"Ils relient plusieurs médias": the people and families present in the ownership chain of at least two media, with how many; each opens its card. "Tous les médias": every title grouped by genre (presse, radio, télévision, en ligne), with its "Éditeur", a "Propriétaire" line only when a source names one ("non publié" in the mockup, see the API tab), "Au sommet" when the chain goes higher, and a "fög" badge when the fög measures it. This list is how keyboard and screen-reader users reach every fact the drawing shows.

{{FIGURE:ui-06-media-bridges-list|Phone screen: Ils relient plusieurs médias with chips Famille fondatrice de TX Group 5, Antoine de Raemy 3, Damien Piller 2, Francis Rivollet 2, Louis-Pierre Roger 2, Thierry De Marignac 2; then Tous les médias, Presse (12), with entries for 24 heures and Tribune de Genève showing Éditeur, Propriétaire and Au sommet|Lists under the graph}}

Signaler une erreur (US-24)

Under the card of a medium, a person or a family: "Signaler une erreur ou demander un retrait" opens the address huni.victor@gmail.com, selectable and as a mailto: link with the name in the subject, and what to write: the medium or person, the error, a source if any. A named person can ask for removal; the request stays private and is handled within 30 days.

Decisions on this layer

Settled
  • A "Transparence" menu group holds "Médias" and a disabled "Lobbies (bientôt)".PRD-0001 §8Mockup v3
  • The media page shows every medium; the territory bar is hidden there and filters nothing.PRD-0001.US-21PRD-0001 §8
  • Two views of the same data with a switch, Réseau and Soleil, and one card shared by the views and the lists.PRD-0001.US-21Research-0003, ruling 5
  • Soleil: a thin unlabeled centre ring, labels on the outer rings only and above a size threshold.Mockup v3, owner review
  • Holder colours: media red, company grey, person or family violet, public body blue, association or foundation green, always with a legend.PRD-0001 §5
  • The fög value under its own label with the media average, never "gauche" or "droite".PRD-0001.US-22Research-0003, ruling 1
  • "Signaler une erreur" uses the owner's address as selectable text and a mailto: link.PRD-0001.US-24PRD-0001 §8
  • The lists carry every fact of the drawing, so the drawing can stay a labelled image for assistive technology.PRD-0001.US-21
Left to the implementation
  • The final wording of the hints, the empty card and the "nouveau" badge, and how long the badge stays.
  • The size thresholds under which a Soleil sector has no label.
  • The layout settings of the network (spacing, repulsion) and its first zoom.
  • Whether the cards of the list open the card in place or scroll to it on a phone.
